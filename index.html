🌙 Moon & Venus ✨

A story written between twilight and starlight.

About the Story

Moon & Venus is a creative project inspired by astronomy, poetry and the emotions hidden within the night sky. The Moon spends each evening searching the sky, waiting patiently for Venus. Though they appear close from afar, they remain separated by distance, time and fate. This story explores devotion, hope and the bittersweet beauty of loving something forever beyond reach.

Artwork

Moon's Monologue

A monologue spoken by the Moon while waiting for Venus.


ill pray in winter ,summer, spring and autumn..

and my hope of your arrival

in amidst of cricket's song & firefly's dance

ill wait all day till the sun goes down

when your first glimpse of love shine..

you'll be the only spark i see

just you and me

close enough to grasp your beauty

what a beautiful cruel curse it is

to be close enough but not reach you.


when sun hides you in his bright blanket,

when the horizon swallow you whole,

i can only count numbers till you return...

even if the world keeps us apart

my eternal devotion and soul

will revolve around you

guide me to your light

Porque eres mi luna.

Symbolism

The Moon represents unwavering devotion.

Venus represents a distant dream — beautiful, radiant and seemingly close, yet forever beyond reach.

The Sun becomes the barrier, separating two celestial bodies that long to share the same sky.

Their relationship symbolizes patience, hope and the quiet persistence of love across impossible distances.

"What a beautiful, cruel curse it is to be close enough, yet never reach you."
